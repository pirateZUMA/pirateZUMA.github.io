About Me

My Story

Welcome! I'm a passionate videographer, artist, and vector image producer. I enjoy taking things that already exist and bringing them to life in different mediums.

Through my creations, I seek to share the emotions I embody and that are experienced by others. What may seem as something small or obselete to others I find joy in, often expressing myself through mediums others have forgotten over time.

My Approach

I believe in the power of a natural approach to things; nothing fully scripted or thought out. Things work best when they fall into place and they often do so without much coaxing.

Whether I am filming videos or making my art, I truly give it my all. No matter the struggle I don't give up; even if others want me to. I improvise easily and learn fast, which makes life easier as a creative mind. Enjoy some of my favorite creations below.
